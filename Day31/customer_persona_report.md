# Day 31 - Customer Persona Analysis

## Objective

The objective of Day 31 was to transform customer clustering results into meaningful customer personas using behavioral data.

## Persona Analysis

### High-Value Customers

- Customer Count: 338
- Average Sales: 20109.22
- Average Quantity: 267.53
- Average Profit: 2616.06
- Average Discount: 0.14
- Average Shipping Days: 3.95
- Average Orders: 75.81

**Persona Description:**

High-value and highly engaged customers with strong purchasing activity.

**Recommended Business Strategy:**

Focus on customer retention, loyalty rewards, personalized offers, and premium recommendations.

### Regular Customers

- Customer Count: 457
- Average Sales: 12792.09
- Average Quantity: 192.31
- Average Profit: 1279.66
- Average Discount: 0.15
- Average Shipping Days: 3.99
- Average Orders: 56.16

**Persona Description:**

Customers with comparatively lower spending or purchasing frequency.

**Recommended Business Strategy:**

Use targeted discounts, personalized promotions, and recommendations to increase purchase frequency.

## Conclusion

Customer personas help businesses understand different behavioral patterns and create more targeted retention, promotion, and personalization strategies.
