# Day 35/60 — Combining Customer Intelligence Modules into One Analytics System

## 📌 Overview

Day 35 focused on integrating the customer intelligence modules developed during Week 5 into one unified analytics workflow.

The goal was to combine customer segmentation, customer personas, product recommendations, anomaly detection, activity risk analysis, and executive dashboard insights into a single decision-support system.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Combine Week 5 analytical modules
- Integrate customer segmentation and anomaly detection
- Integrate product recommendations
- Add customer activity risk analysis
- Create unified business KPIs
- Build an integrated analytics dashboard
- Create a workflow architecture diagram
- Document engineering tradeoffs
- Reflect on the Week 5 sprint

---

## 🧩 Integrated Modules

| Module | Day | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Customer Segmentation | Day 30 | Group customers based on behavior and value |
| Customer Personas | Day 31 | Understand characteristics of customer segments |
| Product Recommendations | Day 32 | Recommend products using customer behavior |
| Anomaly Detection | Day 33 | Identify unusual customer behavior |
| Executive Dashboard | Day 34 | Present customer insights for decision-making |
| Unified Analytics System | Day 35 | Combine the modules into one workflow |
