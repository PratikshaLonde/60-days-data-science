
# Day 42/60 — Building a Unified Customer Intelligence Decision System

## Sprint Review & Business Intelligence Integration

This project combines multiple customer analytics modules into one unified
business intelligence decision-support system.

## Modules Integrated

- Customer Segmentation
- Customer Personas
- Revenue Forecasting
- KPI Monitoring
- Customer Retention Analysis
- Customer Churn Risk Prediction

## Key Features

- Executive KPI monitoring
- 30-day revenue forecasting
- Customer risk analysis
- Customer retention analysis
- Unified customer intelligence workflow
- Executive-level visualizations
- Business decision support

## Business Value

The system helps decision-makers monitor business performance, understand
customer behavior, identify customers at risk, plan future revenue, and
prioritize retention strategies.

## Key Learning

The main learning from Day 42 was understanding how separate analytics
modules can be integrated into one business intelligence workflow.

This project focuses on turning data and analytical results into
actionable business decisions.
