
# Day 42 — Unified Customer Intelligence Decision System

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to combine multiple customer analytics modules
into one unified business intelligence decision-support system.

The system integrates:

- KPI Monitoring
- Revenue Forecasting
- Customer Retention Analysis
- Customer Churn Risk Prediction
- Customer Intelligence Workflow

## 3. Integrated Analytics Modules

### KPI Monitoring

KPI monitoring helps executives understand the current business performance
using revenue, profit, customer, and order-related metrics.

### Revenue Forecasting

The revenue forecasting module uses the Day 37 forecasting results to provide
an expected revenue outlook for the next 30 days.

### Customer Retention

Retention analysis identifies repeat customers and measures the repeat
customer rate.

### Churn Risk Prediction

The customer risk module from Day 41 identifies customers with different
levels of churn risk.

## 4. Business Decisions

The unified system can support decisions such as:

- Monitoring overall business performance
- Planning future revenue
- Prioritizing high-risk customers
- Improving customer retention
- Identifying areas that require business attention

## 5. Business Tradeoffs

### Forecasting

Forecasts support planning, but future revenue can differ from predicted
values because of unexpected business changes.

### Churn Risk

Risk scores help prioritize customers, but they should be treated as
decision-support signals rather than guaranteed churn predictions.

### Retention

Repeat purchase rate provides a simple retention indicator, but it does not
represent a formal cohort-based retention analysis.

## 6. Conclusion

The Day 42 system combines forecasting, KPI monitoring, retention analytics,
and predictive customer risk scoring into one business intelligence workflow.

This unified approach helps transform separate analytics outputs into
business-focused decisions.
