
# Week 6 Reflection — Customer Intelligence & Business Intelligence

## What I Learned

During Week 6, I learned how different customer analytics techniques can
work together to support business decisions.

I worked with:

- Customer segmentation
- Customer personas
- Product recommendations
- Customer anomaly detection
- Executive KPI monitoring
- Revenue forecasting
- Customer retention analysis
- A/B testing
- Customer churn risk prediction
- Unified business intelligence

## My Key Learning

I learned that data analytics is not only about creating models or charts.
The important part is converting analytical results into useful business
decisions.

I also learned how forecasting can support future planning, KPI monitoring
can track business performance, and customer risk analysis can help
businesses prioritize retention actions.

## Challenges

One of the main challenges was integrating outputs from different analytics
modules into one unified system.

I learned that keeping data organized and documenting assumptions is important
when building a larger analytics workflow.

## Business Intelligence

The unified system helped me understand how different analytics modules can
work together to provide a broader view of customer and business performance.

## Final Reflection

Week 6 helped me move from individual analytics tasks toward building a
complete business intelligence decision-support system.

This experience improved my understanding of how data can be transformed into
actionable business insights.
