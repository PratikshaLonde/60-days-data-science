\# Customer Intelligence Platform

\## Capstone Project Proposal — Day 47/60



\### 1. Project Overview



The Customer Intelligence Platform is a data analytics project designed to help businesses understand sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, and customer segments through data-driven insights.



The project will transform raw sales data into useful business information through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, customer segmentation, customer risk analysis, and interactive dashboards.



\### 2. Business Problem



Businesses generate large amounts of sales data but may struggle to identify their most valuable customers, understand unprofitable transactions, monitor sales performance, and make informed business decisions.



This project aims to solve these problems by developing a centralized platform that presents meaningful customer and sales insights.



\### 3. Project Objectives



\- Analyze sales and profit performance.

\- Identify profitable and unprofitable transactions.

\- Segment customers based on relevant business data.

\- Analyze customer risk and retention opportunities.

\- Visualize important business metrics through dashboards.

\- Explore sales forecasting if the available data supports reliable forecasting.

\- Improve the accessibility of business insights through a single platform.



\### 4. Dataset



\*\*Primary dataset:\*\* `cleaned\_dataset.csv`



\*\*Location:\*\* Main ABTalks project folder.



The dataset contains approximately 51,290 rows and 23 columns.



The project will validate the dataset, examine its available columns, handle missing or invalid values where necessary, and select appropriate fields for each analytical module.



\### 5. Target Users



\- Business owners and managers

\- Sales and marketing teams

\- Customer relationship management teams

\- Business analysts



\### 6. Proposed Modules



\*\*Module 1: Data Processing\*\*

\- Load and validate the dataset.

\- Handle missing values and duplicates.

\- Prepare data for analysis.



\*\*Module 2: Sales and Profitability Analytics\*\*

\- Analyze total sales and profit.

\- Compare sales performance across available categories and regions.

\- Identify loss-making transactions.



\*\*Module 3: Customer Segmentation\*\*

\- Group customers according to relevant purchasing and sales characteristics.

\- Summarize the behavior of each segment.



\*\*Module 4: Customer Risk and Retention Insights\*\*

\- Analyze available customer risk indicators.

\- Identify customers or segments that may need attention.

\- Present results as analytical indicators rather than guaranteed predictions.



\*\*Module 5: Sales Forecasting\*\*

\- Investigate historical sales trends.

\- Develop a forecasting approach if the dataset has suitable time-based information.

\- Evaluate forecast accuracy before presenting results.



\*\*Module 6: Interactive Dashboard\*\*

\- Display key performance indicators.

\- Provide filters for relevant business dimensions.

\- Present charts, tables, and actionable insights.



\*\*Module 7: Monitoring and Reliability\*\*

\- Validate input data.

\- Record important events and errors.

\- Document reliability improvements.

\- An interactive dashboard for business analysis.
