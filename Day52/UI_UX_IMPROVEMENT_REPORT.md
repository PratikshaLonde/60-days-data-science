# Day 52/60 — Customer Intelligence Platform UI/UX Improvement Report

## 1. Project Overview
The Customer Intelligence Platform is a Streamlit dashboard designed to help users explore customer data, sales performance, and profitability.

## 2. UI/UX Improvements
- **Professional dark theme:** Provides a consistent dashboard appearance.
- **Sidebar navigation:** Organizes Overview, Sales & Profit, Customer Data, and Business Insights.
- **Interactive filters:** Filters data by customer segment, product category, and order date.
- **KPI cards:** Displays total sales, total profit, unique orders, and unique customers.
- **Clear visualizations:** Makes sales and profit trends easier to understand.
- **Customer data explorer:** Supports searching records and downloading filtered data as CSV.
- **Business insights:** Highlights profitable categories, sales segments, and monthly performance.

## 3. Usability Testing
The dashboard sections, filters, charts, customer search, and CSV download were tested and worked as expected.

## 4. Business Value
The improved interface helps users find information quickly, compare performance, identify trends, and export data for further analysis.

## 5. Technologies Used
- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- Data visualization

## 6. Conclusion
The Day52 improvements make the Customer Intelligence Platform easier to navigate and use by combining interactive filters, key metrics, visualizations, and business insights.
