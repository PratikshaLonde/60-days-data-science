# 60 Days Data Science Journey

## Day 4 – Project Setup & Dataset Selection

### Domain
Sales

### Project Title
Sales Data Analysis

### Problem Statement
Analyze historical sales data to identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing patterns, determine the most profitable products and categories, and identify factors affecting sales and profit.

This analysis will help businesses make better data-driven decisions and improve overall sales performance.

### Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset

### Dataset Source
Kaggle

### Libraries Used
- NumPy
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

### Objectives
1. Analyze overall sales and profit.
2. Identify sales trends.
3. Find the best-performing products.
4. Analyze sales by category and region.
5. Understand the relationship between discount and profit.
6. Identify areas for business improvement.
